# The World, 19 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up the world news of Thursday 19 November
2026, drawn by a program in pure Python standard library: a hand-rolled
5x7 bitmap font, a small vector canvas, and a hand-rolled PNG encoder.

## The day's news

- **Lead:** Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen — the leaders of the
  world's three largest nuclear powers sit down together for the first
  time, at the APEC summit.
- **The Hague:** the World Court delivers its final judgment in the
  Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar.
- **Vice City:** Grand Theft Auto VI rolls out worldwide after 13 years,
  with a 99 on Metacritic and 17M+ copies sold in pre-orders.
- **Washington:** Democrats sweep Congress — Mary Peltola wins Alaska for
  a 56-44 Senate majority, the most seats flipped since 1958.
- **Brussels:** the EU proposes a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine, to be
  repaid only once Russia pays war reparations.
- **Space:** NASA's Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object one
  light-day from Earth.
- **Bay of Bengal:** Cyclone Akvan nears the Indian coast.
- **Canberra:** Australia sanctions Israeli settlers over West Bank
  expansion.

Sources: Reuters, AP, NASA, Financial Times.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes /output/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, deterministic)
```

No network, no wall clock, no files outside the repository — every word
and drawing is baked into `main.py`.
